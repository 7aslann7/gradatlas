# Product direction

## The problem

Master's applicants must reconstruct a decision from scattered university pages, varying grade systems, changing deadlines and uncertain research contacts. They often struggle to distinguish a formal entry requirement from a competitive preference or another student's experience.

## The promise

Help an applicant discover plausible programmes, understand what still needs verification and prepare the next action in one free workspace. A clear link to the university is more valuable than a confident-looking guess.

## Primary journey

1. Explore the map by country, field or research topic.
2. Read requirements and their provenance.
3. Add a profile to identify recorded gaps and review questions.
4. Compare a small set of programmes.
5. Save options, prepare documents and record progress.
6. Draft a specific enquiry when the official page leaves a question unanswered.
7. Complete the application using the university's original portal.

## Product rules

- English throughout the product and contributor documentation.
- Free access to the core product, without premium gates.
- Source dates and applicant categories visible wherever they change a decision.
- No GPA-only "safe", "target" or "reach" labels without a validated, transparent basis.
- No implied rankings, guaranteed admission, invented research openings or fake reviews.
- No automatic mass email. Drafts should improve individual conversations.
- Optional accounts only when they provide a clear benefit; discovery remains available without login.
- Open source code, documented schema and a contribution workflow that is practical to review.

## Early measures of usefulness

Track these through voluntary usability sessions before introducing any analytics: whether applicants can find an official source, understand an unknown requirement, create a useful shortlist and prepare an unanswered question. Also measure correction turnaround and the share of records that remain current.

## Sustainability

Keep a low-cost static discovery layer. Future institutional collaborations, grants, voluntary donations and sponsorships can support maintenance without buying ranking position or restricting essential information. Publish any sponsorship and its separation from discovery order.
