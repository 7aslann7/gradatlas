# Catalogue coverage

Reviewed: **12 October 2026**. Current release: **81 programme and explicitly named specialisation records**, across **34 countries/regions** and **17 broad subject groups**. This adds 65 records to the 16-record pilot.

The catalogue is curated, with uneven geographic and subject coverage. It is not a complete list of universities, a ranking, or a count of all available degrees. Joint degrees are recorded once. A specialisation is identified in its title. Hong Kong is a separate catalogue destination; map locations are approximate host campuses.

Each record links to official university information. 12 records have a source-backed, fully dated application window; this includes closed and future cycles. 41 records include a published professional contact. Missing dates, language thresholds, fees, degree equivalency and attendance information remain explicit. A review date confirms the stated facts were checked, rather than every possible admissions criterion being complete.

## Admission interpretation

Original grade scales and degree-specific requirements are retained. A final-year grade rule, an international equivalency requirement, a selection score, or a published preference is not converted into a universal GPA cutoff. IELTS thresholds name their official source; component scores and exemptions still matter. A funded tuition waiver is distinguished from an institution charging no tuition.

Annual guidance is not rolled forward into a guessed intake. Category-dependent and conflicting dates remain notes when a reliable full window cannot be recorded. Date badges do not prove the portal is accepting applications at the present moment; always verify the official cutoff, local time and applicant category.

## Maintenance

The dataset is `src/data/programs.json`; the subject taxonomy is `FIELDS` in `src/core.js`. Source URLs, coordinates, dates, subject names, delivery modes, grade context and contact provenance are validated before publication. Stable IDs preserve existing shortlists and notes. New contributors should read [the data policy](DATA_POLICY.md) and [the contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
