# GradAtlas v6 Americas/Africa audit


40 additional genuine master’s records across 19 countries, 40 institutions and 39 cities. New-country coverage includes Jamaica, Namibia, Cameroon, Malawi and Zambia. The batch spans 13 existing fields, with 6 architecture/planning, 6 arts/humanities, 4 law, 4 physical-science/mathematics and 3 education records; computing, cyber, engineering, life-science and environment options also deepen the pool. Field totals overlap because some degrees have multiple subjects.

Evidence objects are deliberately limited: 40 degree, 38 duration, 15 mode and 11 teaching-language confirmations. Every evidence URL appears in the record’s sources and has checkedAt `2026-10-12`. An English page or an English admission-test requirement is not treated as proof of English-medium instruction. Institutional language policies are used only where they explicitly describe the university’s instruction language. Unknown metadata remains `Not verified` and is repeated in dataNote.

Validation passed: no duplicate IDs against the 171-record base, existing subject taxonomy only, canonical mode strings, all evidence/contact/IELTS/tuition references present in sources, valid dated-window categories and no numeric GPA conversions. Source-dependent summaries and requirements use short original paraphrases; no individual source is extensively reproduced.

Only two complete current windows were recorded: UCLA MArch for Fall 2027 and Alberta MLIS for Fall 2027. Calls with a precise closing date but no exact opening date have scheduleNote only. Closed 2025/2026 cohorts and recurring yearless deadlines are not relabelled as currently open.

Review notes for the parent:

- Georgetown International Legal Studies has verified degree and admission sources, but duration is left unknown rather than borrowing the London option’s schedule. Zambia Science Education has a blank duration section. Their evidence objects therefore contain degree only.
- Some official pages time out or redirect during direct opening even though the search service supplies the full indexed official content. Stellenbosch’s application page confirms a live 2027 intake. Its detailed prerequisites/part-time duration are supported by the 2025 official yearbook, not a newly invented 2026 rule. The published one-year full-time degree is also supported by the programme site.
- ASU’s typical duration comes from its public departmental handbook. UNAM’s campus mode is supported by an earlier official degree call; this age is visible in dataNote. Other current programme pages establish those degrees’ continuing existence.
- Typical, minimum and maximum periods remain distinct: Stanford average, Purdue typical, USP thesis limit, Queen’s thesis period, and Buea/Makerere limits are not turned into guaranteed completion promises.
- Current Wits entry criteria distinguish normal qualification from the lower-average advice. Purdue’s university baseline is explicitly general, allowing higher department standards. SFU, Manitoba and other scoped native grade rules remain text; no 4-point equivalency is computed.
- Cornell’s tuition label is a sourced fellowship benefit for admitted MFA candidates. It is not marked as a universally zero-fee degree. No other unsupported fee amount or no-tuition statement is included.
- All numeric IELTS scores use programme-relevant official sources. Recommended or generic university scores were not substituted where the programme threshold was unconfirmed.
- TEC’s renamed computing degree is one existing award, not two separate programmes. Its current subject curriculum is verified, while detailed admission criteria remain pending. Rhodes’ detailed current portfolio/qualifying-degree conditions likewise need follow-up rather than extrapolation from undergraduate rules.
- Pretoria is research architecture programme 12252025. Namibia and Jamaica are master’s architecture awards, while preparation, honours and professional-registration pathways remain separate. Uganda’s programme is the full MSc rather than its undergraduate physics major.
- KNUST and Strathmore explicitly mix online and face-to-face sessions, so their mode is Hybrid. Full-time/part-time enrolment alone is never used as mode evidence. Colorado represents a chosen on-campus traditional MS option; its alternative online/professional formats are disclosed.
- Map coordinates are approximate institutional/campus points, not classroom coordinates. Memorial points near its Ocean Sciences Centre; ESPOL and KNUST disclose multi-location teaching or field activity. Remaining delivery locations should be reviewed before portraying a marker as a single mandatory study site.
