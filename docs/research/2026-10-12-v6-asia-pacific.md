# GradAtlas v6 Asia/Pacific and MENA research handoff

Prepared 2026-10-12. Forty additional genuine master's degrees/programmes from ten destinations: Australia (12), Japan (6), South Korea (4), China (5), Hong Kong (2), New Zealand (6), Sri Lanka (2), Nepal (1), Jordan (1), Singapore (1).

All records use the existing 17-field taxonomy. Checked against the catalogue, which had 211 records at final validation: no duplicate IDs or exact degree identities. Broad degrees and their available study routes are represented once rather than inflating the count with separate delivery routes or specialisations.

Field-level evidence is deliberately incomplete: degree 40/40, duration 38/40, language 21/40, mode 14/40. A language-test admission rule is not used as evidence of instructional language. A campus address or full-time workload is not automatically used as proof of campus delivery. Unknown fields are explicit. All evidence, contact, IELTS and fee provenance links are included in each record's sources.

Admission grades remain in original institutional contexts; all comparable-GPA filters are null. Otago, UQ, Sydney, Massey and Auckland grading text must not be converted to a universal 4-point threshold. Colombo's internal progression/award GPA is explicitly separate from entry eligibility. IELTS entries cite observed programme or applicable official admissions rules; conditional/alternative routes are retained where available.

Seven exact dated windows are included only where the official source supplied day boundaries and the relevant cycle/category. Month-only SMU guidance and Waikato's closing-only deadline remain schedule notes, not invented opening/closing windows. Annual guidance has not been rolled into a future intake.

Fee labels with numbers have a tuition.source. Unverified fees remain unknown. Kelaniya publishes an international course fee without an academic-year label; this is stated in the label and note. SMU's August 2027 fee is explicitly indicative.

Material limitations:
- Hiroshima has a current 2027 international master's route, but duration/mode remain unverified; its school structure changes for 2027.
- Several official resources were available only through the official search index while direct fetching failed: GIST degree duration, Kyushu's duration reference, Tsukuba's medical FAQ, Sydney LLM and GJU MSEM. Record notes identify these limitations; no third-party programme source was substituted.
- Wellington's former programme overview URL returned Gone when opened. The current faculty listing and 2026 university calendar corroborate the degree. Keep the overview's extraction caveat visible.
- Colombo describes its first MSc Data Science intake, publishes current dated application guidance and confirms blended delivery. Its first-intake/pre-registration wording and detailed timeline need confirmation.
- SMU currently states an 18-month full-time route, optionally completed in 12 months. Its part-time duration differs between page sections, so the record uses the full-time route and flags the conflict.
- Current Auckland MEngSt explicitly suspends civil, environmental, transport and geotechnical options. The record covers currently listed specialisations rather than suspended ones.
- Melbourne and Massey campus/online options are one degree each; map coordinates show the selected campus.
- Degree-level admissions specifics remain unverified for some programmes; their requirements and dataNote identify the missing checks. “Verified degree” does not mean the full applicant profile is verified.
- All coordinates are approximate campus markers.

No repository source edits, commits, publication, applications or external messages were performed.
