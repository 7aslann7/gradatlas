## Positioning

Master’s programmes, mapped. GradAtlas helps applicants discover programmes, compare a shortlist and prepare enquiries using official-source evidence. The website is free and requires no sign-up. The MIT-licensed source is public; third-party datasets retain their own licences. It is an early public release with partial admissions coverage, not an exhaustive world catalogue or an admission predictor.

## Reddit launch draft

**Title:** I built GradAtlas, a free open-source map for researching master’s programmes. What would make it more useful?

While researching master’s programmes, I kept switching between university pages, spreadsheets and application notes. I built GradAtlas to bring that process into one place.

You can explore a world map, filter by subject and country, compare programmes, track a shortlist and export your workspace to Excel. It also helps prepare editable enquiries with links to official sources. Your profile, notes and saved drafts stay in your browser; there is no sign-up.

This is an early release. It contains 1,489 programme/specialisation records: 358 with programme-page sources and 1,131 Dutch registry identities whose admissions are unreviewed. A separate ROR institution directory supports broader discovery. Missing requirements remain unknown; the university’s current instructions determine eligibility.

I’d especially appreciate feedback on finding programmes, understanding the evidence labels and exporting a useful application plan. What feels confusing? Which official sources or workflows should I add next?

## Outreach and feedback

Start with one relevant community that permits project feedback. Read its current self-promotion rules and adapt the post to its audience. State that you built the project. Avoid repetitive cross-posting, unsolicited messages, artificial votes or promises about admission outcomes. Reddit prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and leaves additional promotion decisions to individual communities. [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam), checked 12 October 2026.

Respond to concrete feedback and track actionable problems through the issue templates. Data corrections need official sources, applicant scope and maintainer review before publication. Use synthetic examples for bugs. Give credit to accepted contributors.

Initial progress can be assessed from substantive feedback, reproducible bugs, reviewed data corrections and repeat interest. There is no analytics integration or download counter in this release; do not imply that traffic or conversion metrics are already collected.
